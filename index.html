Publish approved clips to your own accounts, on schedule.

Pusat Info Publisher is a small publishing tool run by the creator behind Pusat Informasi Viral (TikTok @pusatinformasi_viral, Instagram @pusatinformasiviral). It posts short videos the creator has already edited and approved, at the times the creator chooses.

How it works

  1. The creator connects their own TikTok or Instagram account with the platform's official login (OAuth). The tool never sees the account password.
  2. For each video, the creator reviews a preview, the caption, the visibility setting and the interaction settings (comments, Duet, Stitch), and discloses commercial content when it applies.
  3. Only after the creator explicitly confirms does the tool upload the video through the official TikTok Content Posting API or the Instagram API, at the scheduled time.

Who it is for

This tool is used only by its owner to publish to the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, it does not post on behalf of other people, and it does not read, collect or sell anyone else's data.
