Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Pusat Info Publisher ("the tool", "we") is a personal publishing tool operated by the creator of Pusat Informasi Viral. This policy explains what data the tool handles when a creator connects a TikTok or Instagram account to it.

Data we access

  • Account basics from TikTok (open ID, display name, avatar, and the creator's available posting options such as privacy levels and whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed) and from Instagram (account ID and username). We use these only to show the creator which account a video will be posted to and which settings are available.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok or Instagram through their official login, used only to publish the creator's own approved videos.
  • The videos and captions the creator chooses to publish, and the result returned by the platform (post ID and link).

Data we do not access

We never see or store account passwords. We do not read direct messages, followers' data, or any other person's content, and we do not use data for advertising or profiling.

How data is stored

Tokens and publishing records are stored on the creator's own computer. Videos are held only for the minutes needed for the platform to receive them. Nothing is sold, rented or shared with third parties, other than sending the video and caption to TikTok or Instagram for publishing.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions) or Instagram (Settings → Website permissions / Apps and websites). After revocation, stored tokens no longer work and are deleted from the tool. To request deletion of any remaining records, email us; we act within 30 days.

Children

The tool is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when this policy changes.
